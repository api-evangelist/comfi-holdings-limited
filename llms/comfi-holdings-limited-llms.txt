# Comfi

> Comfi (Comfi Holdings Limited) is a UAE-based financial technology company providing B2B working capital solutions for small and medium enterprises and trading businesses — invoice discounting, Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL), and automotive dealer inventory financing — positioning itself around speed ("capital in hours, not weeks"). No public API or developer program is published as of this profile.

## Products
- Invoice Discounting: convert outstanding invoices into immediate cash
- Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL): flexible B2B credit terms
- Automotive Dealer Financing: inventory financing for vehicle dealerships
